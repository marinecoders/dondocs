Prepare NAVMC 10274 (AA Form)

The Marine Corps Administrative Action form is used for certain routine actions submitted to higher authority. Confirm that your command wants NAVMC 10274 for the action before you start.

Set up the action

Choose New NAVMC 10274 in DonDocs. Enter the action number if required, the date, and the SSIC for the subject. Identify the originator, organization and intended recipient. Fill in Via and Copy to as required by your routing instructions.

State the request

Give the action a specific subject. List the references or authority and any supporting enclosures. Use Supplemental Information to state what you are asking the recipient to do and the facts they need to act. Confirm who completes the processing section and when.

Check the PDF and attachments

Review the exported PDF for complete remarks, readable fields and the correct signature and routing information. Check that each listed enclosure is included in the files you send. Use a document package when you need to assemble the form with supporting files.

Verify current form and routing requirements with the reviewing office before submitting an action.
