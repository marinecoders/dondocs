# Package assembly and delivery

Assembly layout belongs to a saved package revision. Choose a letter-led, forms-led or mixed profile, optional cover and linked contents, then review and save the settings. Saving creates a new revision and preserves previous PDFs. Forms-led output starts with a captured native form as its main document.

Assembly first renders the captured members and measures their actual page counts. It then plans cover and contents pages, shifts physical destinations and creates links and bookmarks. Contents use physical PDF positions; continuation numbers and separate enclosure numbers retain their document sequences. Long contents flow across pages. The saved PDF contents view uses the retained file's captured boundaries.

A cover and contents currently use the built-in Latin PDF font. Unsupported label characters produce an explicit error so the label can be adjusted or the optional front matter disabled. Source PDFs and earlier outputs remain preserved. Assembly does not qualify the combined output as accessible; that requires separate tagged-output and reader checks.

Historical revisions, archived packages and reviewer copies can inspect their saved settings. Change settings from the current owning package. Cancelled, stale and failed saves preserve the last saved revision.

## Same-page endorsements

The optional same-page setting follows SECNAV M-5216.5 Chapter 9 paragraph 1: the complete endorsement must fit on the signature page of the basic letter or preceding endorsement. DonDocs currently qualifies unsigned, unclassified native naval letters and endorsements, on unrotated US Letter pages without annotations or attached enclosure pages. It identifies the printed signer, renders actual page pixels and measures all visible content, including rules and graphics. A 24 pt gap and the 72 pt bottom content margin remain clear.

The complete same-page rendering is embedded without scaling or dropping body content. The preceding page footer remains, and subsequent continuation numbering counts the shared physical page once. A long endorsement, signed source, unavailable canvas, ambiguous signer or unsupported geometry uses a new-page rendering. Any missing fields required by that fallback must be completed before export. The saved output records the placement and reason. Original source files and prior outputs remain unchanged.
